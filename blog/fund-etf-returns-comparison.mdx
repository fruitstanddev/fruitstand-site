## What this actually shows

Most of what's available for funds and ETFs on Snowflake Marketplace isn't
a standalone returns product. **ETF Global** covers fund *flows* (money
moving in and out), which is a different question than trailing or
calendar year performance.

**FactSet** and **Morningstar** do compute fund and ETF returns as part of
their much broader platforms, so it would be misleading to say they can't.
But neither sells that as a self-serve, flat-fee "fund returns" product the
way Fruit Stand does: FactSet's ETF data is licensed through its own
enterprise catalog, and Morningstar's Snowflake listing found under this
search is scoped to equity returns and statistics rather than a dedicated
mutual fund/ETF returns product (Managed Investment Data, the category that
would cover funds specifically, was announced for later in 2026 and we
haven't seen a public listing for it as of this writing).

**CRSP** (now a Morningstar company as of its February 2026 acquisition) is
the closest thing to a direct comparison in spirit: a survivor-bias-free US
mutual fund database built specifically around computed returns, the same
principle Fruit Stand uses to keep delisted funds' history intact. Its
returns are monthly, though, not broken out across the 16 trailing periods
Fruit Stand publishes, and it's distributed as a subscription product to
academic and institutional licensees rather than sold as a flat self-serve
Marketplace listing. 

**Nasdaq eVestment** and **CFRA Research** both
compute returns too, but for different audiences and scopes: eVestment is
built for allocators evaluating hedge funds and alternatives, while CFRA's
ETF data spans a global universe (19,000+ ETFs) with its own STARS ratings
layered on top and a genuinely detailed return schema, daily, 1-month,
3-month, YTD, and 1-year trailing returns, annualized 3/5/10-year and
since-inception returns, plus discrete year-by-year prior returns going
back five years. Where a provider's specific return periods aren't publicly
documented, those cells are marked "Not published" rather than guessed at.
